About

Sognevo is the label I put on the audio software I build. There is no company behind it and nothing for sale: the plugins are free software in both senses. They cost nothing, and the source is on GitHub under the GNU GPLv3, so you can use them on any recording, fork them, or take the code apart to see how they work.

They also don't ask for accounts, don't send telemetry and won't ever grow a paid tier. If something breaks, or you wish a knob existed, open an issue on the plugin's repository.
